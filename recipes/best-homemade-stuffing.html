Best homemade stuffing

"Description"

This best homemade stuffing recipe is my grandmother's turkey stuffing recipe, and it's still the best I've ever had. The stuffing can be cooked inside your turkey or in a baking dish. This recipe makes enough to stuff a very large bird. This turkey stuffing recipe is proof that you can't go wrong with a tried-and-true classic.

"Turkey Stuffing Ingredients"

  • Bread: This old-fashioned turkey dressing recipe starts with lightly toasted white bread.
  • Butter: Butter adds moisture, richness, and flavor.
  • Vegetables: You'll need one chopped onion and two stalks of chopped celery for flavor and texture.
  • Broth: Chicken broth keeps the stuffing moist without making it soggy.
  • Eggs: Two lightly beaten eggs help hold the dressing together and add moisture.
  • Water: You can add a few tablespoons of water, if you'd like, to achieve your desired consistency.
  • Seasonings: This turkey dressing recipe is seasoned with salt, pepper, rubbed sage, and garlic powder.

"Ingredients"

  • 30 slices white bread, lightly toasted
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 1 large onion, finely chopped
  • 2 stalks celery, finely chopped
  • 2 cups chicken broth
  • 2 large eggs, lightly beaten
  • 2 tablespoons water, or as needed (Optional)
  • 2 teaspoons rubbed sage
  • teaspoon garlic powder
  • salt and pepper to taste

"Steps"

  1. Preheat the oven to 300 degrees F (150 degrees C). Spread the toasted bread slices in a single layer on baking sheets. Bake until completely dry and crisp, 20 to 30 minutes, turning once. This helps dries out the toast so it can absorb the broth without turning soggy. Remove from the oven and let cool slightly.
  2. Preheat the oven to 325 degrees F (165 degrees C). Lightly grease a 9x13-inch baking dish.
  3. Crush toast into crumbs with a rolling pin; transfer to a large bowl.
  4. Melt butter in a medium saucepan over medium heat. Add onion and celery; cook until soft, 5 to 7 minutes. Remove from heat; drain.
  5. Stir broth and eggs into bread crumbs until moist, but not soggy. Add water, if needed. Fold in onion and celery, sage, and garlic powder; season with salt and pepper.
  6. Press mixture into the prepared baking dish.
  7. Bake in the preheated oven until top is brown and crisp, about 1 hour.
